## Invite share — [#32](https://github.com/themark-net/leasegrid-c/issues/32) (shipped in source)

`83b3e66` — `feat(sync): shell polish with short-code invite share (Fixes #38, Fixes #32)`.

Join → Storage servers → Offer / store → Credit is the lead path. The share card is a short code, a QR of that code, and **Copy code**, on Offer and on Join. The raw introducer `pb://` string is not the primary share. **Show full join link (advanced)** stays collapsed. #27 paste-a-server-link and #34 Credit under More are unchanged.

That commit is **not** in the v0.1.0 installer files. Those files still share a join link (**Copy link** / **Create invite / Copy invite**). Join on v0.1.0 already accepts a short code when someone has one. There is no installer for `83b3e66` until a later tag, and this draft is not that tag.

## Offer pie — [#28](https://github.com/themark-net/leasegrid-c/issues/28)

`fcb4c35` — `feat(sync): separate Offer hosted usage from the disk pie (Fixes #28)`.

The disk pie is this computer’s used, kept, and offered space. Usage hosted for other people is a separate strip. The chip opens More → Credit. #30 was out of scope for this commit.

## Credit top-up — [#34](https://github.com/themark-net/leasegrid-c/issues/34)

`442b76e` — `feat(sync): swap Credit faucet stub for XMR top-up (Fixes #34) (#36)`.

**More → Credit → Top up** is quote, pay, then redeem against a lab issuer. CI uses FakeChain. A stagenet wallet-rpc is used when that is configured. Mainnet is refused.
